# Architecture handoff

## Sources and scope
Identify supplied evidence in any medium, its version, the current design question,
confirmed inputs, assumptions, exclusions and next consumer.

## Functional and quality requirements
Record current behavior, applicable quality dimensions/scenarios, source and priority,
observable criteria, verification methods and unresolved questions.

## Views
For each selected context/container/component/deployment view record editable source,
scope, identity mapping, current version, applicability and actual render/review coverage.
For an applicable code-level zoom, identify the parent component and link relevant classes,
interfaces/functions and their origins. Record whether the view is proposed or source-derived.

## Decisions
Link inputs to architectural choices or retention and the affected view IDs/elements,
alternatives, responsibility/data/
dependency allocation, benefits, costs and verification strategy.

## Detailed-design handoff
State ready results, unresolved architecture-changing blockers, target modules/interfaces,
constraints for interactions/state design, and the actual consumer. Separate mechanical
checks, visual review, semantic assessment and formal acceptance. Upstream changes
invalidate affected choices until checked again.
