## Local adaptations

- The ten source-inspired checks are expressed as operational actions and evidence requirements, not verbatim excerpts.
- Formal readiness uses evidence eligibility first. Missing applicable evidence remains **Not fully verified**; a clean preparation script cannot supply missing implementer or independent-review evidence.
- The actual author/verifier identity defines independent review. A skill change or repeated self-audit remains the same actor unless a distinct reviewer is involved.
- Package severity is about the consequence for design readiness; it is not a conversion into IEEE operational hazard levels.
- Triage percentiles, structural warning thresholds, equivalent-page conventions and scheduling assumptions are local heuristics. No threshold of complexity, universal CDR duration or universal expected defect density was established by the inherited source review.
- Zero findings is valid. Assess coverage and meaningful negative regressions; RITME yield is not a finding quota or a proof that a clean review was superficial.

## Historical validation record

The upstream record distinguished independent review rounds from self-audits and disclosed that a planned complete quotation-to-original comparison was not run. Those checks concerned an earlier package. They do not certify the current instructions, parser correctness, legal clearance or commercial fitness.

The 2026-10-29 revision removes long source transcriptions, aligns authorization and evidence rules, and corrects the shipped positive examples. Current engineering validation is recorded separately in the remediation acceptance report. It is not a fresh audit of every book/standard citation.
